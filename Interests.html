Rock Climbing

El Capitan

This is El Capitan, "El Cap" for short. It's a massive granite monolith in Yosemite National Park, California, which rises approximately 3,000 feet from the valley floor. It is Recognized as one of the world's most famous big-wall climbing destinations. Alex Honnold is the only person in history to free solo the full route of El Capitan. On June 3, 2017, he became the first individual to complete this feat without ropes or safety gear, ascending the Freerider route (graded 5.13a) in 3 hours and 56 minutes.

Hiking and Backpacking

Scotchman's Peak

There's not much like hiking through a hailstorm that feels like full-auto airsoft blasting every square inch of you. The hail was excellent at getting into your pockets and shoes. Add a thunderstorm on top of that at the peak, and you've got a solid hike. Great Views. Highly recommend. Best hiked in the winter.

Winter Camping

Winter camping always beats summer camping. There's no bugs, no one cares about the massive fire you made, and there's a free dishwasher all around you (the snow). Food, warmth, and shelter always feel so much sweeter when you have to work harder for it to fight the cold. Cold is just better. Never forget to bring your bread dough and honeybutter for breakfast lunch and dinner.
